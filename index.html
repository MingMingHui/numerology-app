Help & FAQ

What is numerology?

Numerology is the study of the symbolic meaning of numbers and their supposed influence on human life, based on your birth date and the letters of your name. This app uses the Pythagorean system, the most widely used method in the Western world, which assigns each letter of the alphabet a number from 1–9.

How are the calculations done?

Every number is built by summing digits or letter-values and reducing the result to a single digit (1–9) — unless a Master Number (11, 22, 33, 44) appears, which is preserved for its amplified significance. Every calculation in your report includes a full step-by-step breakdown so you can verify the math yourself.

What information should I enter?

Use your full birth name exactly as it appeared on your birth certificate — not a nickname or married name — for the most traditionally accurate reading. Enter your birth date exactly as recorded, including the correct year.

What are Master Numbers?

11, 22, 33, and 44 are considered "Master Numbers" carrying heightened potential and challenge. They are not reduced further during calculation (e.g. 29 → 2+9 = 11 stays at 11, not reduced to 2).

What are Karmic Debt Numbers?

13, 14, 16, and 19 are considered Karmic Debt numbers. When one of these appears as the very first total in a calculation (before reducing), it points to a lesson carried over from a past pattern that this lifetime is here to resolve.

Is my data private?

Yes. This entire application runs client-side in your browser. Your name and birth date are never transmitted to any server — the only storage used is your browser's local storage, purely so you can revisit your last report.
